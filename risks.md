## Comparable Texts Analysis

### Comparable Text 1 — *Bayesian Statistics the Fun Way* (Will Kurt, No Starch)
- **Target reader / context:** beginners wanting Bayesian intuition; self-study.
- **Strongest:** approachable, intuition-first.
- **Weakest:** single-paradigm; no frequentist comparison; no method-choice framework; no LLM implementation.
- **Why choose IT:** gentler if the reader only wants Bayesian thinking.
- **Why choose THIS:** teaches the *choice* between paradigms and modern implementation.

### Comparable Text 2 — *Statistical Rethinking* (Richard McElreath, CRC)
- **Target reader / context:** graduate applied-Bayesian course; full code.
- **Strongest:** rigorous, comprehensive Bayesian modeling.
- **Weakest:** graduate-level, code-heavy, Bayesian-only; not an undergraduate on-ramp.
- **Why choose IT:** the right next course after this one.
- **Why choose THIS:** undergraduate entry point that compares paradigms first. Positioned as the step *before* McElreath (named as the onward pointer in Ch 13).

### Comparable Text 3 — *Bernoulli's Fallacy* (Aubrey Clayton)
- **Target reader / context:** readers persuadable that frequentism is broken.
- **Strongest:** sharp critique of NHST.
- **Weakest:** polemic, not a how-to; one-sided; no implementation.
- **Why choose IT:** for the argument.
- **Why choose THIS:** even-handed and practical — names where frequentist methods genuinely win and teaches both.

## Top 3 Adoption Risks

1. **Field-stability of the LLM material.** The book's modern hook (LLM
   implementation) is also its fastest-aging part. Mitigated structurally by
   quarantining prompt-specific content — but it must be maintained.
2. **The comparative structure vs. single-paradigm syllabi.** The book's
   central differentiator is also its biggest adoption friction in departments
   that teach one paradigm. Needs a clear supplementary-use path and possibly a
   frequentist-only reading order.
3. **Companion-website dependency for the capstone.** Ch 12 — the chapter that
   demonstrates the whole thesis — cannot run without the dataset library.
   Lowest-effort contingency: ship a static dataset bundle even if the
   interactive site slips.
